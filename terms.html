Conditions for using this personal-use Windows calendar synchronization utility.

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Purpose

Outlook Calendar Sync is a personal desktop utility that synchronizes events from an authorized Outlook calendar to a designated Google Calendar.

Authorized use

Users must have permission to access and synchronize the calendar information concerned and must comply with any applicable workplace, organization, Microsoft, and Google policies.

Synchronization effects

The utility may create or update Google Calendar events. The user is responsible for reviewing configuration, keeping appropriate backups, and verifying results. Outlook source events are intended to remain unchanged.

The developer may modify or discontinue the tool. Users may stop using it and revoke the Google authorization at any time.
